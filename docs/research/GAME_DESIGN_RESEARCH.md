# Product-structure tournament and game-design synthesis

**Purpose:** compare the plausible human-to-world structures on one rubric, test the preferred thesis against the blind ECHOHOUSE challenge, and define the evidence basis for product authorities.

**Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE FOR SYNTHESIS — scores are planning judgments, not player evidence

**Authority boundary:** this file owns the tournament method, scores, sensitivity, and objections. Product and game authorities own accepted behavior. The coordinator alone records final decisions and source-ledger rows.

**Related documents:** [competitors](COMPETITORS.md), [player research](PLAYER_RESEARCH.md), [distribution research](DISTRIBUTION_RESEARCH.md), [model research](MODEL_RESEARCH.md), [zero-anchor challenge](../reviews/ZERO_ANCHOR_CHALLENGE.md), [product](../product/PRODUCT.md)

## Recommendation

Select **H, the bounded-region hybrid**: sponsor one autonomous citizen inside one small canonical region; relationships, descendants, institutions, artifacts, reputation, and the patron covenant outlive the citizen. The human makes rare, informed interventions and returns to visible changed conditions plus a factual Chronicle. The 52-hour proof is local-first, eight citizens, and one settlement; public canon and private World Forks remain later gates.

**PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS:** attachment to one citizen can make systemic consequences matter without turning play into either unit control or passive log reading.

The blind challenger, **X/ECHOHOUSE**, is the strongest rejected alternative. It concentrates drama and immediate replay better, but its required authored-crisis cadence, disposable worlds, and model-first whisper mechanic are weaker on no-model operation, continuity, and session-20 depth. Its critique is accepted: the first slice must concentrate the focal cast, force a legible decision boundary, and prove causal comprehension before expanding society.

## Gate audit

| Required gate | H evidence or implementation rule |
|---|---|
| Consequential first-session agency | Counsel at one decision boundary changes the citizen's plan or creates an explicit refusal; delayed consequence resolves by minute ten |
| Non-log return value | Open on changed world tableau and unresolved action; Chronicle explains but is not the whole return |
| Emotional continuity after death | Successor selection inherits covenant, relationships, artifacts, reputation, and unfinished commitments; never a reset |
| Newcomer relevance | Focal low-status citizen has current stakes; mature history is progressive disclosure, not required reading |
| Deterministic no-model operation | Standard Brain completes every decision and all replay; models are optional proposal renderers later |
| Difference from SEED | one sponsor relationship rather than multiple owned workers; checkpoint absence rather than real-time obligation; factual causality rather than conversation volume; local free proof rather than shared paid MMO economy |
| 40–60-hour proof | eight citizens, three resources, four behavior families, one exchange, one repair/conversion, one counsel chain, IndexedDB, one Chronicle; no server/generalized economy |

## Strongest objections and uncertainties

1. **PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS:** eight deterministic citizens may read as clockwork rather than persons. Gate B must fail if players cannot explain a choice as a citizen's value/belief/relationship, even when replay is correct.
2. **PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS:** indirect counsel may feel weak. The player must forecast stakes and see both immediate interpretation and delayed authoritative effect.
3. **UNRESOLVED:** the minimum visual performance needed to notice interaction without reading text. The rendering spike and fresh observer gate decide.
4. **UNRESOLVED:** lineage continuity may be intellectually clear but emotionally reset. Do not implement death in the slice; paper-test succession first.
5. **UNRESOLVED:** session-20 richness is a structural claim, not proof. Do not build month-two content before first-ten return/attachment evidence.
6. **INFERENCE:** Chronicle polish can launder trivial simulation. Every sentence must trace to events, and product review must judge the underlying decision before presentation.

## Rejected alternatives and reopen evidence

- Reject direct control because it removes the defining shared agency; reopen if most fresh testers ask for possession after understanding counsel.
- Reject family/trio/faction as starting unit because each dilutes one-person responsibility or adds management; reopen if one-person tests fail attachment while identical family/trio tests pass.
- Reject shared civilization for the first slice; reopen only after both product gates and a separately approved server/operations budget.
- Reject ECHOHOUSE as foundation; reopen under the randomized advantage defined in sensitivity or if bounded-region play cannot deliver a complete consequence by minute ten.
- Reject model-branded citizens, unrestricted dialogue, and foundational local-model download; reopen only after blinded story-quality and return tests while Standard Brain remains complete.

## Constraint fit and resulting implementation behavior

The winning structure deliberately proves only a local single-save semantic canon. It uses no accounts, deployment, hosted inference, training, proprietary data, regulated data, payments, partners, or enterprise motion. The first slice spends implementation complexity on one visible citizen decision, causal state, persistence, and return—not on governance depth, economic breadth, lineage simulation, public canon, or private forks.

## Handoff

**Findings:** H is robust across declared sensitivities; X supplies valid concentration and causal-legibility requirements; one citizen beats family/trio/faction for the starting responsibility unit.

**Objections:** indirect agency, clockwork behavior, and Chronicle laundering remain fatal if player evidence confirms them.

**Uncertainties:** attachment, voluntary return, session-20 depth, and succession are unvalidated product hypotheses.

**Recommendation:** implement only Gate A/Gate B evidence described above before any public-world, lineage-depth, or model work.
