# EONFOLK bounded naming screen

**Purpose:** Record a narrow collision screen for the provisional EONFOLK codename before it is used throughout planning.

**Status:** SCREENED FOR PLANNING USE — 2026-08-20. This is not legal clearance, a registrability opinion, or evidence of common-law availability.

**Authority boundary:** This document owns naming-screen evidence and the planning-codename recommendation. [Decisions](../decisions/DECISIONS.md) owns acceptance; no document authorizes filing, purchase, or public launch.

**Related documents:** [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md), [product](../product/PRODUCT.md), [design](../design/DESIGN.md), [risks](../decisions/RISKS.md).

## Owned decision

Retain **EONFOLK** as the private planning codename. The exact fused word produced no result in the checked public code-hosting, game/app-store, RDAP, USPTO, WIPO Madrid, or EUIPO TMview queries. The components “Eon” and “Folk” are crowded ordinary terms, so absence of an exact hit is weak evidence and not a clearance conclusion.

## Evidence that reopens the decision

- Any exact or confusingly similar entertainment, game, software, simulation, or social-world mark discovered in a professional knockout search.
- A platform listing, company, domain use, or repository with prior public use in an adjacent category.
- Counsel identifies confusion risk from EON-formative marks despite the fused word's exact-result screen.
- Real-user pronunciation tests show persistent confusion with an established product.
- The project moves from a private experiment toward a public commercial brand, filing, domain purchase, or paid promotion.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Claim the name is clear:** rejected; exact-query absence cannot establish trademark or common-law availability.
- **Buy a domain now:** rejected; no purchase or spend is authorized and a domain does not clear a mark.
- **Rename preemptively:** rejected; no material exact collision was found in this bounded screen.
- **Treat the GitHub repository as priority evidence:** rejected; repository creation is hygiene, not trademark priority or clearance.

## Remaining uncertainty

Phonetic, translated, design-mark, company-name, unregistered/common-law, class-specific, and jurisdiction-specific conflicts remain unsearched or incompletely searched. Search interfaces and records change. The general and app-store searches can miss unindexed or region-restricted products. A public launch requires a fresh human review; commercial use may justify qualified legal advice.

## Resulting implementation behavior

- Use `EONFOLK` as a codename in private planning and the private repository.
- Do not place ™/® symbols, file an application, acquire a domain, publish a store page, or make a clearance claim in this run.
- Re-run exact and similarity searches immediately before any public naming commitment.
- Keep naming isolated from simulation/protocol identifiers so a later rename is a presentation change, not a migration.

## Constraint fit

The screen used public read-only sources and existing tools, incurred no approved spend, requested no credential, created no partnership or filing, and adds no work to the 52-hour gameplay slice.
