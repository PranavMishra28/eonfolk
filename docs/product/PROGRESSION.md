# Progression, continuity, and session depth

**Purpose:** define what accumulates across sessions, a citizen life, succession, and eras without turning EONFOLK into a content or number treadmill.

**Status:** LONG-HORIZON PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS — not a V1 claim

**Authority boundary:** this file owns progression meaning and continuity. [AGENT_LIFE](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md) owns citizen state; [WORLD_STRUCTURE](WORLD_STRUCTURE.md) owns topology; [HUMAN_LOOP](HUMAN_LOOP.md) owns session timing.

**Related documents:** [product](PRODUCT.md), [Chronicle](CHRONICLE.md), [governance](../game/GOVERNANCE.md), [economy](../game/ECONOMY.md)

## Owned decision

Progression is **richer context and changed obligations**, not patron level, idle yield, territory, citizen slots, or command frequency. Six durable strands can accumulate: relationship history, sourced beliefs, Standing Plans/commitments, reputation, artifact ownership/provenance, and institution precedent. A seventh—lineage/covenant succession—activates only after attachment is proven.

| Horizon | New depth | Explicitly not progression |
|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | recognize one person; cause/allow one consequence | tutorial rewards, skill tree |
| Session 5 hypothesis | predict an interpretation; repair/regret a remembered act | more clicks, faster gathering |
| Session 20 hypothesis | use relationships, precedents, roles, artifacts, and era history to evaluate a new risk | generalized economy or content grind |
| Month two hypothesis | mature institutions constrain strategies; newcomers can challenge inherited assumptions; one controlled shock counters equilibrium | infinite scale, daily obligation |
| Death | choose witness/successor; inherit obligations and social consequences; preserve obituary and unfinished plan | reset, paid life, random replacement |

## Deferred death and succession contract

Death never occurs during the first session or because the app was closed. A forewarned boundary records direct cause, contributing conditions, last Standing Plan, relationships, offices, possessions, reputation, and covenant status. The next active decision is whether to sponsor an eligible descendant/associate, remain an unbound witness, or defer once. Successor access is not genetic omniscience: inherited items/obligations are facts; memories require testimony/artifacts.

The sponsor history may survive as a social record, not magical control. A successor may refuse it. This needs a paper/click comparison before any implementation or player-facing promise; current documentation is not evidence that care transfers.

## Inactive sponsor and stopping

On close, the save records a stable checkpoint and the citizen's Standing Plan. Return advances only through deterministic catch-up boundaries; stable spans coalesce and death/shortage/ownership/plan-expiry shocks interrupt. No emergency notification, streak, or punishment. An inactive sponsor's citizen continues by Standard Brain and can revise an expired plan; they do not freeze awaiting the player.

## Mature-world newcomer and stagnation

A newcomer begins through one current person and current tension, with progressive disclosure of only relevant historical facts. They receive no lesser “late join” economy in the local proof. Later shared canon should offer unfilled civic needs, outsider relationships, recoverable artifacts, contested precedents, and apprenticeship—not empty land or a reset shard.

Canonical stagnation is diagnosed when no meaningful role, ownership, relationship, plan, scarcity, or institution changes over a configured era. The response is a bounded, rules-grounded pressure already in the world model (seasonal shortfall, office expiry, maintenance failure, migration), not an LLM-authored catastrophe or arbitrary wipe. The slice includes only a seeded shortage.

## Rejected alternatives

Reject patron XP, purchasable lives/slots, compulsion streaks, wall-clock decay, infinite resource scaling, collectible model personalities, raw transcript memory, and content drops as the sole session-20 plan. Reject death as the slice's attachment shortcut.

## Reopen evidence

Reopen if players remember system growth but not people; succession tests feel like reset; mature-world onboarding requires excessive history; or first-20 prototype sessions exhaust meaningful combinations before relationships/precedent add depth. Then simplify the long horizon or choose episodic structure before adding breadth.

## Remaining uncertainty

**PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS:** contextual mastery sustains session 20. **UNRESOLVED:** the best successor relationship and fair warning horizon. **UNRESOLVED:** whether a controlled shock feels systemic rather than authored manipulation.

## Resulting implementation behavior

The slice persists relationship deltas, belief provenance, one Standing Plan, and branch-specific precedent because Gate B needs them. It implements only the first outcome-dependent return choice. It does not claim or implement session 5/20, death, lineage, month-two shocks, offices, or mature-world entry.

## Constraint fit

Typed histories reuse the event log and avoid a separate progression/content backend. The first proof stays eight citizens, local, $0, no model, no training, and free. No revenue, paid lives, data custody, partnership, or enterprise feature is implied.
