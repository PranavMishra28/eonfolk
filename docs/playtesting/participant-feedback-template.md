# Participant feedback template

Optional. The participant may skip any item. No name.

Copy answers into `tmp/playtesting/` only.

```text
Participant ID: P__
Commit (filled by moderator): ______________

In your words, what was this?

Which person or event do you remember?

When you sponsored someone, what did you think “sponsor” meant?

Did the person have to take your advice? What makes you say that?

After the Chronicle: what is a fact in this world, and what is only
someone’s belief or accusation?

If you opened this tomorrow, what would you try to find out?

Circle one, then write why:
  I would return because something is unresolved.
  I might return, but the reason is weak.
  I would stop here.

Optional (1 = not at all, 7 = very much; skip if you do not want numbers):
I could tell what people were doing.     1 2 3 4 5 6 7
I could tell my advice was not control.  1 2 3 4 5 6 7
The Chronicle helped me separate fact
from belief.                             1 2 3 4 5 6 7

Anything confusing, broken, or inaccessible (keyboard, motion, reading)?
```

Numeric answers are optional and secondary. They are not proof of enjoyment
or attachment.
