# Moderator guide

Say as little as possible during the core task. You are watching whether the
running product teaches the loop, not whether you can narrate it.

## Consent and privacy

- Ask once: they may stop at any time; notes are local; no account is created.
- Do not require a name. Use `P01`-style IDs only.
- Do not collect employer, email, or sensitive demographic data.
- Do not upload notes, recordings, or world dumps.
- If they share a screen recording, keep it in `tmp/playtesting/` and treat it
  as private.
- Do not paste private world contents into issues.

## Before they sit down

- World is already running at the loopback URL.
- You know how to reach Mara, sponsor, counsel, abstain, and Chronicle so you
  can recover a stuck session *after* the silent observation window.
- You will not hint at “the correct” counsel.

## What you may say

Allowed, once, if they ask what this is:

> It is a small local settlement. People act on their own. You can watch,
> sponsor one person, and offer rare advice. They do not have to take it.

Do not say that you control Mara, that the Chronicle is omniscient, or that a
model is required.

## What you record

Use [observation-template.md](observation-template.md). Keep four columns
honest:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observed | What they did or said, timed if possible |
| Quote | Exact words, in quotation marks |
| Inference | What you think it means, labeled as yours |
| Interpretation | Later synthesis, after the session |

Never promote an inference to an observation. Do not call “I might come back”
retention.

## After the session

Thank them. Offer to delete the local world (clear site data) if they used
your browser profile. File only anonymized, high-level bugs through the
ordinary issue forms if a mechanical defect appeared.
