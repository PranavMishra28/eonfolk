# Session script (10–20 minutes)

Commit: ______________ Date: ______________ Participant ID: P__
Prior exposure: none / saw a clip / played before
Viewport: desktop / laptop / mobile (physical or resized)
Input: pointer / keyboard / mixed

Do not tell them the “right” counsel. Keep Parts 1–3 mostly silent.

## Part 1 — Cold observation (about 4 minutes)

Give them the running product with no architecture lecture.

Ask:

1. What do you think you are looking at?
2. What are people in this place doing?
3. Choose or follow one person. What do you think they want or are dealing
   with right now?

Record: first confusion, first meaningful click, time to orient, words they
misunderstand, whether the picture or the text carried understanding.

## Part 2 — Sponsor decision (about 5 minutes)

Ask them to sponsor the intended citizen (Mara Vale) and either offer counsel
or intentionally abstain.

Ask, before the outcome lands:

1. Why that choice?
2. What do you think this person will do?
3. Do you expect them to obey you?

Record whether accept / reject / delay / reinterpret is legible after it
happens. Do not correct their prediction.

## Part 3 — Consequence and Chronicle (about 5 minutes)

After the later consequence and Chronicle:

1. What happened?
2. Why do you think it happened?
3. Which part was caused by your intervention?
4. Which part merely happened before or around it?
5. What did the citizen believe?
6. What is the Chronicle claiming as fact?

Record whether they independently use, or clearly distinguish, direct cause,
trigger, contributing condition, temporal order, and allegation versus
verified fact. Do not tutor the vocabulary unless they ask.

## Part 4 — Memory and return intent (about 3 minutes)

1. What person or event do you remember most?
2. What would you do if you opened this again tomorrow?
3. Is there anything you want to know about this settlement?
4. Did you feel like you controlled the citizen, advised them, or watched
   them?
5. What would make you return?

Do not ask only “did you enjoy it?” Open questions first. Optional numbers
are on the participant sheet.

## Closing

If they want to stop, stop. If a control is broken, note it as a defect and
continue only if they agree. Offer to clear local site data.
