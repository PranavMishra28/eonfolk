## Gate 0 — Product kill gate

Before anyone is exposed, freeze both the six-treatment surface and observer projection in one artifact/study. Six participants see all six branding-hidden ugly versions: `H` selected one-citizen Riverhold, `FAM`, `TRI`, `FAC`, `ECH`, `DIR`, using the Goal prompt's fixed chooser/authority/continuity contracts. Use every balanced Williams row: `H FAM DIR TRI ECH FAC`; `FAM TRI H FAC DIR ECH`; `TRI FAC FAM ECH H DIR`; `FAC ECH TRI DIR FAM H`; `ECH DIR FAC H TRI FAM`; `DIR H ECH FAM FAC TRI`, mapped P01–P06 in row order. Each treatment occupies every ordinal position once and follows every other treatment once across the cohort. Hold visible facts, relationship, three choices, information, cost, fidelity, 90-second decision, 45-second post-confirmation consequence, 15-second view, Replay response, fixed 225-second slot, and 60-second reset constant. After each decision ask the exact Desirable/Continue binaries, predicted consequence and confusion/objection; after consequence ask diagnostic-only `Would you replay this consequence now?`; record meaningful-action time. Finally rank 1 most to 6 least, no ties. Any withdrawal, protocol-invalid attempt, or skipped presentation fails. Missing Desirable/Continue remains raw null while scoring No; Replay may remain raw null and is diagnostic. Pass requires `H` to have the unique lowest rank sum and at least 4/6 Yes on Desirable and Continue; Replay is not part of the floor. A tie/loss, failed absolute floor, or any alternative exceeding H by at least two true responses on either scored binary reopens. Five separate observers complete the frozen visual rubric; only the combined eleven-record source may be imported/analyzed, after both groups finish. Frozen keep, all-zero-floor-fail, and comparative-reopen mocks produce identical reviewer results.

## Gate A — Proof of Life

The named build shows eight Standard-Brain citizens, three resources, four legible behavior families, one story-relevant exchange/repair loop, and one two-citizen interaction that changes authoritative state. All five fresh silent observers use one frozen Gate A application commit, seed, and simulation-time fixture; only the Gate 0 observer questions/rubric are reused, never its discarded prototype. Without log/narration, at second 60 they point to Mara, name three citizen activities, and identify both interaction participants plus the authoritative change. At least 3/5 pass that whole rubric. No model or external-egress path exists.

After action/timeout and before the other condition, ask the four comprehension prompts, then contingency from 1 `fixed/unrelated` to 7 `strongly depended on facts/values/relationships/advice`, and desire to take the next Riverhold action from 1 `not at all` to 7 `very strongly`. Missing rating, withdrawal, or either protocol-invalid condition fails with no imputation/replacement. The real build includes fixed Mara, state-changing investigation by 60 seconds, two advice intents plus abstain by five minutes, branch-specific interpretation/consequence, explicit leave/advance/return, one outcome-dependent second choice, and a factual three-beat/≤20-second Chronicle/card.

Pass requires:

- on the real condition only, 6/8 select and confirm advice/abstain by five minutes using only frozen UI;
- on the real condition only, 5/8 correctly answer the immediately administered branch-rubric prompts for Mara's decisive visible reason, whose choice it was (`hers`), later branch event, and save location (`this local browser/device`);
- on the real condition only, 4/8 initiate the branch-legal second action within 60 seconds without prompting and select the exact Mara/Toma concern, curiosity, obligation, or anticipated-relationship-consequence token rather than reward/technology/other;

One branch/card is selected and committed before exposure by the Goal prompt's exact `cardSeed` rejection rule; all five view that card. The operational branch is abstention, so the correct adviser is `no-one`, no advice/contribution/rule change may be claimed, and the consequence is ledger uncertainty remaining while Mara follows her plan.

The 6/8, 5/8, and 4/8 rules are independent counts over the same eight real-condition records; they need not be the same participants. Control-condition equivalents are diagnostic only. Both conditions still supply the ratings used by the strict mean comparison.

## Severity and stop behavior

- **P0:** data loss/corruption, unauthorized mutation, secret exposure, unplayable journey, or factual fabrication. Stop and fix.
- **P1:** any gate/correctness/replay/access/budget/comprehension failure. Fix before readiness.
- **P2:** bounded material friction with workaround and reopen trigger.
- **P3:** polish with no gate effect.

No accepted P0 or unmitigated P1 remains. At each later integration and final runnable HEAD, every passed gate must reproduce the frozen observable oracle exactly; a mismatch cannot preserve the historical PASS. A new P0/P1 after final review gets one fix and targeted confirmation pass; unresolved means not ready.

## Rejected alternatives and reopen evidence

Reject build-only acceptance, one favorable observer, self-testing as human evidence, fixed-script success, raw logs, model-backed theater, and timebox expiry. Reopen the thesis if the irreducible early investigate → advice → divergence → return choice cannot fit 60 focused hours after declared cuts, or the real loop fails to beat its scripted control.
