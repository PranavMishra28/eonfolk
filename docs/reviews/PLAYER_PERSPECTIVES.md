## Walkthrough traces

### Nontechnical stranger

- **Landing / 5 seconds:** attractive but unclear whether to create, choose, or wait; sponsor/patron/covenant language implied money or lore.
- **30 seconds / entry:** moving people were not yet a reason to care; fixed Mara needed one plain action and autonomy line.
- **Interaction / advice:** wanted two clearly different choices plus abstain, plain evidence, and a humane receipt when Mara refused/reinterpreted.
- **End / return:** a cliffhanger without a playable next response felt manipulative; silent time advance and local-only storage weakened trust.
- **Chronicle / card / replay:** factual beats were the strongest feature if evidence was expandable and technical IDs hidden; would not send a dead link/seed.

### Systemic-game player

- **Landing / 5 seconds:** colony signals implied resource control that the product later denied.
- **30 seconds / entry:** one focal citizen could work, but the tournament had not used equal interactive controls.
- **Interaction / advice:** one seed and one low-cost advice looked solver-dominant/scripted; routine needs made clockwork, not personality.
- **End / return:** wanted a second counterplay decision with an actual stake; one reload was not session depth.
- **Chronicle / card / replay:** causality was useful only if different decisions produced different state vectors and stories.

### AI-native skeptic

- **Landing / 5 seconds:** “autonomous” was an unsupported marketing claim until behavior beat a canonical script.
- **30 seconds / entry:** fixed Mara was more honest than “create a mind”; first interaction had to mutate authoritative state.
- **Interaction / advice:** demanded trust/value/evidence/commitment perturbations, transfer to another citizen, baselines, ablations, and typed decision receipts.
- **End / return:** immediate reload proved storage, not voluntary return or attachment.
- **Chronicle / card / replay:** causal homecoming was the honest hook; V1 had no modern-AI-specific value and should say so.

### 45-second mobile visitor

- **Landing / 5 seconds:** loading plus eight tiny figures could consume the promise; Mara/CTA needed semantic paint before Pixi.
- **30 seconds / entry:** panel takeover, overlapping targets, and create/sponsor ambiguity predicted abandonment.
- **Interaction / advice:** distinctive independent behavior arrived too late; wanted a state-changing investigate by second 45.
- **End / return:** local-device limitation needed disclosure before investment, not after counsel.
- **Chronicle / card / replay:** 9:16 card could work if it showed advice/choice/consequence without a dead link or seed jargon.

### Creator/streamer

- **Landing / 5 seconds:** too many visual facts competed under stream compression; no recipient landing route existed.
- **30 seconds / entry:** no framing cue identified the important pair; fixed Mara contradicted create/select language.
- **Interaction / advice:** three-to-ten-minute delay created dead air; stable readable advice options could support ordinary chat without integration.
- **End / return:** creator needed a manifest-reproducible leave/advance reveal and a real next action.
- **Chronicle / card / replay:** one 20-second audit/friendship clip was strong, but one immutable spoiler, false causal card labels, and no recipient route were not a creator loop.

## P0 findings against the frozen state

### PP-001 — P0 — A single favorable scripted trajectory cannot prove agency or attachment

The frozen acceptance could pass with a canonical Riverhold lookup, one seed, one handpicked observer, and factual recall. The AI skeptic required state perturbation, transfer to a non-Mara actor, Standard-Brain baselines/ablations, and a randomized yoked scripted control with at least eight unfamiliar formative sessions. The creator separately found that all runs could converge on one spoiler (`AP-001`, `CP-002`).

**Minimum correction:** three materially different terminal world vectors from one snapshot; perturb trust/value/evidence/commitment/advice; transfer fixture; compare real versus scripted control; all failures in denominator.

### PP-002 — P0 — The frozen “share” artifact had no recipient path

The card promised a canonical URL/click reason while implementation excluded deployment/public routes. A seed was useless to a stranger. This could not prove distribution (`CP-001`, `MP-011`).

**Minimum correction:** either authorize/build a no-sign-in recipient route in a later goal or relabel V1 as private **Copy story card** comprehension evidence with no dead link, seed jargon, or acquisition claim.

### PP-003 — P0 — Session-20 value was documentation, not slice scope

The frozen plan invoked offices, lineage, precedents, eras, and mature institutions while implementing one scenario and excluding most of those systems (`CP-003`).

**Minimum correction:** label 001 as a single Riverhold proof and remove session-5/20, succession, newcomer, and retention implications from V1 acceptance/copy. A later gate must implement and observe those claims.

## P1 findings against the frozen state

### PP-004 — P1 — Arrival contradicted itself about create, choose, assign, sponsor, patron, and witness

All five perspectives saw a false or confusing choice. The frozen slice actually had fixed Mara. On mobile, the ambiguity likely consumed the first 20 seconds (`MP-003`, `CP-006`).

**Minimum correction:** one action, **Follow Mara**, one line, **She acts for herself**. Explain the limited sponsor relationship; no create/roster/candidate language.

### PP-005 — P1 — The differentiating action arrived after likely abandonment

Advice could take ten minutes; early observation read as screensaver/case file/dead air. Mobile predicted exit by 45 seconds; creators could not sustain narration (`MP-009`, `CP-004`).

**Minimum correction:** useful Mara shell by two seconds, CTA by three, state-changing investigate preview/action by 30/60 seconds, genuine advice by five minutes, and an optional verified pre-boundary rehearsal fixture for QA—not a skipped-facts player shortcut.

### PP-006 — P1 — Eight clockwork citizens and one scenario could masquerade as autonomy

The systemic player saw chores rather than personalities; the skeptic required same-pressure personality changes and controls. Seeded randomness alone was insufficient.

**Minimum correction:** authoritative citizen interaction, typed actual decision terms, trust/value/evidence/commitment perturbations, nearest-need/legal-random/canonical-lookup baselines, field ablations, and transferred scenario.

### PP-007 — P1 — The return cliffhanger had no next playable loop or reproducible time contract

The frozen Chronicle asked repair/escalate but provided no second decision. Leave/return timing was not creator-rehearsable and silent wall-time mapping was unclear (`CP-008`).

**Minimum correction:** explicit **Leave Riverhold at checkpoint**, manifest interval, user-confirmed **Advance Riverhold**, changed world first, and one branch-dependent second action with authoritative effect.

### PP-008 — P1 — The portrait flow likely became tiny people behind a nested-scroll dashboard

Mobile found panel dominance, overlapping canvas targets, no collision rule, 200% zoom/back risk, and the five-second load budget consuming orientation (`MP-001`, `MP-002`, `MP-004`, `MP-005`, `MP-006`).

**Minimum correction:** world ≥55%; peek ≤35% and non-scrolling; one Inspect scroll; sticky Close/Back/Continue; semantic shell/CTA before renderer; overlapping targets open a named chooser; persistent People route; 200% zoom and browser-Back tests.

### PP-009 — P1 — Local-only persistence could feel like a trust-breaking surprise

Participants could invest before learning there was no cross-device restore and storage could be lost (`MP-010`).

**Minimum correction:** **Progress stays on this device** in first peek and before advice; surface blocked storage before commitment; verified export is read-only and honestly says V1 cannot import it.

### PP-010 — P1 — Story copy gave the sponsor false causal credit

`YOU ADVISED → MARA DID → RESULT` visually implied the player enacted the law, despite counsel being only a contributing condition (`CP-007`).

**Minimum correction:** **YOU ADVISED → MARA CHOSE → WHAT FOLLOWED**; qualifiers remain in compact layout; evidence opens event IDs/types; viewer test rejects direct-credit misunderstanding.

### PP-011 — P1 — The frozen Gate B measured recall, not care

One player could reconstruct events without warmth, responsibility, voluntary next action, or cross-surface identity. The systemic player would not reach session 20; the nontechnical stranger had little return intent.

**Minimum correction:** eight unfamiliar sessions, person-centered reason, voluntary outcome-dependent second choice, consistent Mara world/portrait/return/Chronicle identity, and randomized real-versus-scripted comparison. Do not call immediate reload retention.

### PP-012 — P1 — Spectator participation was an attractive but undeclared expansion

Bounded A/B/C/abstain options were naturally pollable, but no spectator-readable pause existed (`CP-005`).

**Minimum correction considered:** stable readable decision pause and one-line stakes work for the player and ordinary screen-sharing. Do not add chat/Twitch integration or creator dashboard to V1.

### PP-013 — P1 — The first 10 users need a coherent primary learning cohort

Five broad audiences in one tiny sample would make failures hard to interpret. High-touch invitations are appropriate, but evidence should distinguish formative product sessions from marketing/channel tests.

**Minimum correction:** first run the eight unfamiliar randomized product/control sessions under one script; record persona notes as covariates, not separate success thresholds. Distribution cohorts occur only after product proof and publication authority.

## Cross-persona consensus

All perspectives preserved the same narrow hook: a limited player advises one person; she does something other than obey; an audit changes public record while a friendship bears the cost; the return explains exactly what happened. They rejected “AI town,” raw agent activity, generic colony chores, model branding, and a log as sufficient value.

The strongest five-second phrasing is:

> I advised Mara to expose a shortage. She checked first. The audit found no theft; the rule changed, and the friendship broke.

It is one branch-specific hook, not proof that every run or session 20 is rich.
