# EONFOLK frozen game and attachment red team

**Purpose:** Hostilely test whether the frozen EONFOLK synthesis defines a fun, fair, non-managerial, zero-model game loop that can create attachment and return within the binding solo 40–60-hour slice.

**Status:** COMPLETE — **NOT READY**; nine P1 findings require reconciliation before Goal mode.

**Authority boundary:** This review owns independent game-loop, attachment, progression, fairness, and player-test objections against frozen commit `4f47eae8fe785f3994e053d01c184e9e3dddb401`. It changes no product authority and makes no implementation decision.

## Verdict

**NOT READY.** The synthesis has a disciplined simulation and truth architecture, but the playable proof can currently pass by presenting a polished, factual account of one substantially predetermined vignette. That does not yet prove a game.

The most likely first-player reaction is: “I watched eight task tokens, read a compact case file, gave Mara the advice the scenario expected, then the game narrated a council outcome.” The strongest return hook—repair the friendship or enforce the precedent—is not an implemented return verb. The strongest long-term defenses—succession, institutions, mature-world entry, and session-20 context—are excluded from the slice and have no mandatory pre-implementation player test. Standard Brain correctness can pass while all eight citizens still look like the same priority queue with different labels.

No P0 is established in the planning state. That is not a Chronicle-factuality pass: no running Chronicle exists yet. Nine P1s can produce a Gate A or Gate B false positive and must be fixed in the authorities and acceptance plan before implementation starts.

## Findings

### GR-001 — P1 — Riverhold is a staged vignette that can launder trivial choice

**Evidence and location.** [Human loop, “Decision-boundary requirements”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#decision-boundary-requirements) requires at least two legal actions with materially different forecasted stakes. [Chronicle, “Fixed Riverhold evidence chain”](../product/CHRONICLE.md#fixed-riverhold-evidence-chain) specifies only one patron counsel—“Expose the shortage before the council vote”—followed by the immutable RV-005 through RV-012 sequence. [Governance, “Owned decision”](../game/GOVERNANCE.md#owned-decision) explicitly makes the petition threshold and vote a fixed fixture. [Game systems, “Proof scenario”](../game/GAME_SYSTEMS.md#proof-scenario) calls the outcome scenario-scoped. [001 Milestone 2](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md#milestone-2--gate-b-proof-of-attachment-16-hours) requires tests for accept/reject/delay/reinterpret branches but does not require different downstream world states for different counsel intents.

**Failure mechanism.** The build can force each response label in a fixture, resume the same audit/petition/law cascade, and then truthfully Chronicle that cascade. Sentence-to-event traceability proves the summary did not lie; it does not prove the player's advice, Mara's personality, or the underlying simulation created a nontrivial choice. A fixed vote after a fixed petition is authored plot encoded as events. Chronicle polish then makes a shallow state machine look like emergence.

**Minimum fix.** Specify a small Riverhold branch table before code: at least three materially different intents plus abstain; the visible stakes for each; the value/belief/relationship conditions that produce acceptance, delay, rejection, or bounded reinterpretation; and at least two different authoritative terminal states affecting relationship, resource access, or rule state. Do not require every branch to pass the law. Remove RV-010–RV-012 from the slice if the institutional branch cannot genuinely diverge; Mara/Toma's relationship consequence is the stronger and cheaper proof.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Five fresh players see the same pre-choice state. Before choosing, at least four can distinguish the state direction of all presented intents. Across controlled runs, at least three counsel/abstain choices produce at least two mechanically different terminal states and no two differently worded options collapse to the same state. Players first inspect the changed tableau without Chronicle; at least four identify one actual change. Chronicle is revealed afterward, and all five can separate what counsel contributed from what Mara and the council did independently.

**Affected authorities.** [PRODUCT](../product/PRODUCT.md), [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [CHRONICLE](../product/CHRONICLE.md), [GAME_SYSTEMS](../game/GAME_SYSTEMS.md), [GOVERNANCE](../game/GOVERNANCE.md), [EVALS](../quality/EVALS.md), [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [D-003/D-004](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [R-001/R-003](../decisions/RISKS.md), [Q-002/Q-004](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-002 — P1 — The first three minutes are a screensaver plus case-file UI

**Evidence and location.** [Human loop, “Time contract”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#time-contract) gives the player labels at 0–5 seconds, task silhouettes at 5–30 seconds, and identity/values/relationships/tension plus fact/belief/allegation inspection through minute three. The first consequential boundary can arrive as late as minute ten. [Player research, principle 3](../research/PLAYER_RESEARCH.md#3-responsibility-and-identification-support-appreciation-not-necessarily-moment-to-moment-fun) says appreciation cannot excuse a passive first session and recommends a reversible low-stakes intervention before the consequential one. [001 Exact scope](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md#exact-scope) includes only one Riverhold decision boundary.

**Failure mechanism.** The player is asked to admire autonomy, read a dossier, and learn epistemic categories before doing something satisfying. “Observe” changes nothing. “Investigate” is an information transaction whose cost and refusal are abstract. This resembles debugging or management inspection, not play. The autonomous-citizen thesis makes waiting intentional, but intentional waiting is still boredom if the citizen's first visible acts are resource errands.

**Minimum fix.** Put one bounded, reversible Investigate decision between 30 and 60 seconds: choose a source, place, or witness; show the citizen react and move toward it; and make the result alter what the player can responsibly counsel later. Compress the opening vocabulary to ordinary language such as “seen,” “heard from Iven,” and “unverified.” Defer “Standing Plan,” confidence bands, causal-parent types, and governance terminology until they are needed. This is not a second command economy; it is the first playable question.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Five fresh players receive no facilitator explanation. All five perform a purposeful Observe/Investigate action within 60 seconds, and at least four can say what question they are trying to answer. By minute three, at least four can name Mara, what she wants, who matters to her, and why the grain count is uncertain without opening a raw log or repeating internal taxonomy. Record abandonment and facilitator rescue; either is a failure, not a missing data point.

**Affected authorities.** [PRODUCT](../product/PRODUCT.md), [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [PLAYER_RESEARCH](../research/PLAYER_RESEARCH.md), [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [Q-001/Q-002](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-003 — P1 — Gate A can pass with eight clockwork citizens

**Evidence and location.** [Game systems, “Owned decision”](../game/GAME_SYSTEMS.md#owned-decision) reduces visible life to needs, movement, three resources, four behavior families, one exchange, and one repair. [Agent life, “Decision cycle”](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md#decision-cycle) and [Cognition, “Standard Brain”](../engineering/COGNITION.md#standard-brain) define scored templates with seeded tie-breaking, but no authored citizen choice matrix or repetition bound. [Quality Gate A](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md#gate-a--proof-of-life) passes when an observer names three activities and notices an interaction. [Evals, “Behavioral rubric”](../quality/EVALS.md#behavioral-rubric) supplies qualitative questions but no first-slice threshold for distinguishing citizens or detecting repetitive schedules.

**Failure mechanism.** Eight copies of the same need/plan priority queue can gather, consume, exchange, and repair in perfect deterministic order. A bilateral exchange qualifies as a citizen interaction even if relationships never meaningfully alter either participant's decision. Different names, portraits, task silhouettes, and public justifications can then cosmetically personalize identical policies. The player learns the scheduler, not Mara.

**Minimum fix.** Before UI work, author an eight-citizen behavior matrix containing at least two same-state counterfactuals where values or relationships change the selected legal action, one interrupted-plan case, one socially costly help/refusal case, and explicit repetition/oscillation limits. Make the visible exchange or help action causally depend on a relationship or commitment. Fold generic resource activity into Riverhold where possible, so behavior that proves life also produces the focal tension instead of running beside it.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Show five blinded reviewers two short silent clips with names and portraits hidden, then reveal compact value/relationship cards. At least four can connect a material action difference to a visible value, commitment, or relationship and predict which of two citizens will choose which action in a counterfactual replay. A three-minute run must show no citizen stuck in an obvious two-action loop and no single behavior family occupying the whole focal window. The same seed must replay exactly; changed relevant Mind state must change at least one selected action.

**Affected authorities.** [GAME_SYSTEMS](../game/GAME_SYSTEMS.md), [AGENT_LIFE](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md), [COGNITION](../engineering/COGNITION.md), [EVALS](../quality/EVALS.md), [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [D-005](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [R-002](../decisions/RISKS.md), [Q-003](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-004 — P1 — Rejectable counsel has no fair uncertainty contract

**Evidence and location.** [Human loop, “Retained verb contracts”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#retained-verb-contracts) says the UI displays a value, belief, or relationship basis, while a citizen may accept, reject, delay, or reinterpret. [Agent life, “Decision cycle”](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md#decision-cycle) permits citizen-private facts and beliefs in `DecisionContext`. [Cognition](../engineering/COGNITION.md) makes one deterministic winning proposal but does not define which response factors are knowable to the sponsor before commitment. [Open question Q-002](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q-002--does-refusal-preserve-autonomy-without-destroying-agency) promises “likely interpretations,” yet neither the human loop nor `001` defines a preview, deterministic threshold, frequency, recovery opportunity, or bound on reinterpretation distance.

**Failure mechanism.** If a hidden belief flips acceptance to refusal, the player experiences arbitrary denial. If every deciding factor and outcome is shown, “autonomy” becomes a solved menu. If reinterpretation can travel too far from the selected intent, the game steals the player's only rare intervention. If outright refusal leaves no immediate follow-up, a ten-minute session ends because the game said no. Merely showing a justification after the result does not make the risk fair.

**Minimum fix.** Define a response contract with three layers: factors visible before commitment; explicitly marked uncertainty; and factors that may remain private but cannot alone reverse the displayed forecast. Bound reinterpretation to a named intent family and display its plausible range. For the first slice, permit outright rejection only when the pre-choice UI exposes the blocker and a meaningful response remains; otherwise use acceptance, delayed acceptance, or bounded reinterpretation. Specify outcome distribution over the authored fixtures so branch tests are not manually forced labels.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Five fresh players run two matched Riverhold states that differ by one visible belief/relationship fact. Before counsel, at least four predict the response class or marked uncertainty; afterward, at least four explain why Mara responded and how her action remained inside the promised intent range. No player should describe the result as random, cheating, or the same outcome with different wording. A rejection must expose an immediate meaningful alternative—investigate, abstain, repair, or wait with a concrete checkpoint—rather than dead time.

**Affected authorities.** [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [AGENT_LIFE](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md), [COGNITION](../engineering/COGNITION.md), [EVALS](../quality/EVALS.md), [D-003](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [R-001](../decisions/RISKS.md), [Q-002](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-005 — P1 — The return promise has no playable second loop and no session-5/20 depth

**Evidence and location.** [Product, “Why return”](../product/PRODUCT.md#why-return-without-making-the-chronicle-the-game) and [Human loop, “Time contract”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#time-contract) promise repair, escalation, investigation, commitment, or trust on return. [Human loop, “Resulting implementation behavior”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#resulting-implementation-behavior) defers Commit and implements only Observe, one Investigate, Counsel, and Abstain. [001 Milestone 2](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md#milestone-2--gate-b-proof-of-attachment-16-hours) implements one Riverhold decision boundary. [Progression](../product/PROGRESSION.md) assigns session-5/20 depth to remembered repair, precedents, roles, artifacts, era, and lineage, while its slice behavior defers death, lineage, offices, and month-two shocks. [World structure, “Stagnation behavior”](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md#stagnation-behavior) gives the slice only the seeded Riverhold shortage and no adaptive pressure system.

**Failure mechanism.** After the single audit/vote chain, the player returns to a changed tableau but cannot actually make the advertised next choice. The Chronicle becomes the return activity the plan says it rejects. Routine gathering and needs continue, but no mechanism produces a second context-rich human decision. Session 5 is replaying chores after authored content is exhausted; session 20 exists only because deferred nouns are listed in a table.

**Minimum fix.** Choose one honest path. Either narrow the slice claim to a single-session choice prototype and remove return/session-5/session-20 readiness language, or add one second, smaller decision boundary immediately after return using the same relationship and rule state—for example, repair with Toma versus spend standing to enforce the precedent. Before code, run the Q-007 compressed state test and specify how at least five sessions produce new tradeoffs from accumulated existing state without adding a new rule family each session.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** After completing Riverhold and reloading, five fresh players land on the changed tableau. Within 30 seconds, at least four identify and can take a consequential action that is not opening Chronicle or clearing a notice. In a compressed five-session walkthrough, at least three can name a remembered regret/repair and predict how Mara will interpret the next choice. A session-20 paper or browser-state test must expose at least two materially different current choices caused by accumulated relationships/precedent/artifact state, with no new mechanic invented for that session.

**Affected authorities.** [PRODUCT](../product/PRODUCT.md), [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [PROGRESSION](../product/PROGRESSION.md), [WORLD_STRUCTURE](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md), [CHRONICLE](../product/CHRONICLE.md), [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [D-001/D-003](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [R-001/R-003](../decisions/RISKS.md), [Q-007/Q-008](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-006 — P1 — Death replaces the learned person and calls the remainder continuity

**Evidence and location.** [Progression, “Death and succession contract”](../product/PROGRESSION.md#death-and-succession-contract) says a successor inherits items/obligations as facts but not memories and may refuse the covenant; the player can then remain an unbound witness or defer once. [Agent life, “Life, death, and succession”](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md#life-death-and-succession) confirms the successor is a distinct socially linked person, not a cloned mind. [Tournament](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md) credits H with death continuity via covenant, relationships, institutions, and inheritance. [Open question Q-006](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q-006--does-succession-feel-like-continuity-rather-than-reset) admits the reset risk and proposes a paper test, but [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md) excludes death/lineage and schedules no mandatory succession preflight.

**Failure mechanism.** The player's accumulated skill is learning how Mara interprets counsel. Death deletes that relationship target and her private history. What survives is mostly a Chronicle, obligations, and world state—the exact log-and-management residue the product is trying to avoid. A successor's refusal can remove the core sponsor verb entirely. Calling this “continuity” is structurally true and emotionally unproven.

**Minimum fix.** Make the Q-006 paper/clickthrough test a prerequisite to implementation readiness, not a later note. Specify the emotional pacing before the successor menu, the playable rights of an unbound witness, how a refusal produces a new relationship opportunity rather than a dead end, and exactly three inherited hooks that change the successor's first choice without transferring private memory. Compare the same aftermath through one-citizen, family, and trio lenses.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Five fresh players first complete a short pre-death Mara scene, then receive the same causal death and successor aftermath. At least four identify the successor's own current motive and one inherited obligation without reading a full biography; at least three describe the next decision as continuity rather than restart or replacement; all distinguish inherited fact/testimony from Mara's private memory. A refusal branch must still expose a meaningful player role within 30 seconds.

**Affected authorities.** [PRODUCT](../product/PRODUCT.md), [PROGRESSION](../product/PROGRESSION.md), [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [AGENT_LIFE](../game/AGENT_LIFE.md), [WORLD_STRUCTURE](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md), [tournament](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md), [D-001](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [Q-006](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-007 — P1 — Inactive-sponsor catch-up has no player-facing time source

**Evidence and location.** [World structure, “Boundaries”](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md#boundaries) says simulation time advances by commands/scheduler/catch-up and never uses wall clock as simulation authority. [Progression, “Inactive sponsor and stopping”](../product/PROGRESSION.md#inactive-sponsor-and-stopping) says return advances through deterministic catch-up boundaries. [Human loop, “Time contract”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#time-contract) names day and week returns. The simulation catch-up table defines algorithms for 10 minutes, 24 hours, seven days, and 90 days, but the reviewed product/game authorities never define what converts real absence into a requested simulation gap, whether the player can preview/decline it, or which shock ends the first return.

**Failure mechanism.** If real elapsed time silently chooses the gap, the product recreates wall-clock pressure at the application layer even though Reality is deterministic. If every return advances a fixed amount, “While You Were Away” is staged theater. If the player chooses an arbitrary horizon, the day/week attachment claims are misleading. In every case, a shortage or relationship shock can be delivered before the player understands the exposure, and long catch-up can turn return into a progress screen.

**Minimum fix.** Define one explicit boundary: noncanonical elapsed time may propose, but never silently authorize, a capped `AdvanceWorld` command. Show the maximum simulated advance, the current Standing Plan, and named interruption classes before commitment; stop at the first salient shock; allow cancel/resume only at committed checkpoints; and prohibit first-return death or irreversible covenant loss. A player-selected fixed horizon is also acceptable if the day/week language is rewritten honestly.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** With a fake clock, close/reopen the same seed after nominal 24-hour and seven-day gaps. Repeated runs reach identical hashes/checkpoints, stop at the same first salient boundary, expose a reason, and can cancel/resume without divergence. Five players can state how much may advance, whether closing caused any punishment, and what will stop catch-up before they commit. No one waits through an unbounded progress screen or receives an irreversible loss without warning.

**Affected authorities.** [WORLD_STRUCTURE](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md), [PROGRESSION](../product/PROGRESSION.md), [HUMAN_LOOP](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md), [simulation](../engineering/SIMULATION.md), [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [Q-010](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-008 — P1 — One citizen “won” an unequal paper tournament

**Evidence and location.** [Tournament, “Base result”](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md#base-result) assigns H a 4.33 planning score, and [“Identical anchor-unit comparison”](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md#identical-anchor-unit-comparison) declares one citizen selected over family, trio, and faction. Those variants are short prose descriptions, not matched interaction prototypes or player results. H receives retention credit for lineage, institutions, and era depth that the slice excludes, while family/trio are penalized for scheduling/attention behavior that no bounded counsel-only prototype tests. [D-001](../decisions/DECISIONS.md#d-001--bounded-region-citizen-sponsorship) turns the judgment into the accepted starting structure. [Q-001](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q-001--does-one-sponsored-citizen-create-attachment) compares family/trio only after one-citizen failure.

**Failure mechanism.** The chosen unit may be the cleanest camera but the weakest playable relationship. A bounded family or trio can make counsel visibly contested, make succession native, and provide multiple perspectives without job scheduling. Locking the state model, UI, and Riverhold staging to Mara before the cheap comparison biases later evidence toward sunk-cost preservation.

**Minimum fix.** Before production code, build the same six-card or ugly-browser Riverhold scene in four lenses: one citizen, family, trio, and faction. Hold facts, number of interventions, consequence delay, and presentation time constant. Do not add work allocation to the family/trio variants unless equivalent management is intrinsic to the one-citizen version. Test responsibility, character recall, perceived agency, management leakage, and next-session desire; use the result to keep or reopen D-001.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** At least six fresh players see two randomized matched variants each and never see the structure labels. Preserve one citizen only if it is not materially worse on “my input mattered,” remembered person, and desired next step, while being clearly better on “I was managing units.” Include the faction lens as a lightweight storyboard if a browser variant is too costly, but record it in the same rubric rather than assigning another expert score.

**Affected authorities.** [PRODUCT](../product/PRODUCT.md), [tournament](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md), [D-001](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [R-001](../decisions/RISKS.md), [Q-001](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

### GR-009 — P1 — The attachment gate can pass on one handpicked success

**Evidence and location.** [Quality Gate B](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md#gate-b--proof-of-attachment) requires only “at least one fresh player” to state decision, interpretation, later change, and next concern. [001 Milestone 2](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md#milestone-2--gate-b-proof-of-attachment-16-hours) repeats that threshold. [Open question Q-001](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q-001--does-one-sponsored-citizen-create-attachment) requires three of five to recall the focal identity/value/tension. [Player research, “First 10 completed sessions”](../research/PLAYER_RESEARCH.md#first-10-completed-sessions) proposes 8/10 identity, 7/10 causal reconstruction, 6/10 next-outcome curiosity, and 4/10 voluntary return. Gate A similarly allows one observer.

**Failure mechanism.** One enthusiastic or unusually systems-literate tester can pass implementation readiness while four others are bored, confused, or feel ignored. The plan tracks no Gate B threshold for abandonment, facilitator rescue, unfair refusal, or preference for direct control. The stronger evidence is postponed until after the plan can already declare the slice complete.

**Minimum fix.** Align implementation readiness with a five-player minimum: retain every start and abandonment in the denominator; forbid facilitator rescue; separately ask identity, forecast, interpretation, causal chain, unfairness, and next-step questions; and record individual responses rather than an aggregate “observer passed.” Keep seven-day return as later product validation, but do not call Gate B attachment on one success.

**Browser/player acceptance test.** Five fresh players start from a clean local save. At least four reach a consequence unassisted within ten minutes, name Mara and her present motive, and reconstruct player input → Mara interpretation → later change. At least three volunteer a concrete next concern without options. Zero factual misread caused by the UI may survive; all abandonment, “the game ignored me,” and direct-control requests remain visible in the evidence package.

**Affected authorities.** [QUALITY_BAR](../quality/QUALITY_BAR.md), [EVALS](../quality/EVALS.md), [PLAYER_RESEARCH](../research/PLAYER_RESEARCH.md), [D-002/D-003](../decisions/DECISIONS.md), [Q-001/Q-002/Q-004](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), and [001](../exec-plans/active/001-foundation.md).

[Chronicle](../product/CHRONICLE.md) supplies one fixed share card from one fixed Riverhold chain.

The five-second comprehension test is still worthwhile, but it is not evidence of intrinsic distribution or activation.

Test both with five unfamiliar viewers; at least three identify actor/input/consequence in five seconds and can explain why the two histories differ. Treat “would inspect this outcome” separately from “understood the card.”

### GR-011 — P2 — Mature newcomer relevance is asserted outside the product being built

**Evidence and location.** [World structure, “Newcomer relevance”](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md#newcomer-relevance) promises a low-status current citizen, causally relevant history, vacancies, apprenticeship, migration, contested precedent, and artifacts. [Progression, “Mature-world newcomer and stagnation”](../product/PROGRESSION.md#mature-world-newcomer-and-stagnation) repeats the promise. The local proof has one owner/save, eight fixed citizens, no public canon, no migration, no office system, and no mature-world entry path.

**Failure mechanism.** The tournament receives credit for mature-world accessibility from systems that do not exist in the slice. “Low-status entrant” can also make a late player feel deliberately subordinated. Progressive disclosure is a UI intention, not proof that old precedents produce a legible current choice.

**Minimum fix and test.** Mark mature newcomer as a post-gate hypothesis rather than evidence for V1. Before any public-region decision, load a compressed mature save into a semantic newcomer view. Five fresh players should find the current person/tension in 60 seconds, need no more than three historical facts for the first choice, and never request empty land or a reset to understand their role.

**Affected authorities.** [WORLD_STRUCTURE](../product/WORLD_STRUCTURE.md), [PROGRESSION](../product/PROGRESSION.md), [tournament](../research/GAME_DESIGN_RESEARCH.md), and [Q-007](../decisions/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

### GR-012 — P2 — V1 does not decide whether Mara is selected, created, or assigned

### GR-013 — P2 — Investigate hides an energy mechanic behind “attention”

**Evidence and location.** [Human loop, “Retained verb contracts”](../product/HUMAN_LOOP.md#retained-verb-contracts) charges “one bounded attention opportunity and simulation time,” allows witness refusal, and provides no replenishment, cap, preview, or reason why another source remains available.

Adding an undefined attention opportunity makes clue gathering feel like an energy meter or adventure-game guess: choose the wrong witness, receive incomplete evidence, and lose the only meaningful pre-choice action. ### GR-014 — P3 — Patron, sponsor, covenant, and witness are four emotional frames

**Evidence and location.** The authorities alternate among “sponsor,” “patron,” “covenant,” and “witness,” while [Player research, “Unproven assumptions”](../research/PLAYER_RESEARCH.md#unproven-assumptions) explicitly leaves the relationship frame unresolved.

## Strongest surviving choices

- **Mara verifies before disclosing.** This is the best concrete expression of autonomy: recognizable counsel is preserved, obedience is avoided, and the social cost remains attributable. It should become one real branch, not the only scripted path.
- **Fact, belief, allegation, ownership, custody, and accessibility remain separate.** The Toma reserve is a strong truth test because “hidden from open bins” is not theft. This materially protects player trust.
- **Standard Brain is the complete game.** No key, account, model download, branded personality, training, or hosted call belongs in this slice. Fix behavior design before reaching for prose fluency.
- **Abstain and clean stopping are real verbs.** No streak, emergency alert, idle reward, or wall-clock death is a sound attachment posture.
- **One bounded town and eight authoritative citizens are the right implementation ceiling.** The objection is not population size; it is that routine activity must reveal people rather than merely fill the map.
- **Changed tableau before Chronicle is the right hierarchy.** Enforce it with a no-Chronicle comprehension test so the summary cannot carry the game.
- **Relationship cost plus a public consequence is a promising dual stake.** Preserve the duality, but let different advice genuinely change which cost/outcome occurs.
- **Local deterministic replay and typed provenance support both trust and useful debugging.** They should serve the game, not become the game.
