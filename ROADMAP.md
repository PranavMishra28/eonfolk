# EONFOLK roadmap

EONFOLK is pre-alpha. This roadmap describes outcomes to prove, not promised dates.
The current priority is a small, complete local game before any hosted platform.

## Now — prove one living world

- Make eight citizens' work, movement, exchange, repair, and social interaction
  understandable without reading a raw event log.
- Complete the sponsor loop: investigate, intervene or abstain, observe an
  independent response, see a later consequence, reload, and verify the causal
  chain in the Chronicle.
- Keep simulation, persistence, catch-up, and replay deterministic with every
  external model unavailable.
- Pass the critical player journey in production builds at 1728×1117,
  1366×768, and 390×844, including keyboard and reduced-motion paths.
- Keep the initial route within the recorded payload and frame-time budgets, and
  document any device class that has not been measured.
- Make a fresh clone install, build, test, and run with the documented toolchain.

**Exit evidence:** one exact source revision passes the full test lattice and an
independent review finds no unresolved release-blocking correctness, security,
accessibility, or player-experience defect.

## Next — test attachment with people

- Run a small, consented invite playtest with systemic-game players, AI-native
  skeptics, nontechnical observers, and creators.
- Measure whether new players can identify three citizen activities, understand
  one social interaction, and create or follow a citizen in the first minute.
- Measure whether players can predict plausible intervention responses, explain
  the later consequence, and choose a second action after returning.
- Test whether a 10–20 second factual replay communicates the consequence and
  its three causal beats to someone unfamiliar with EONFOLK.
- Test the semantic fallback and representative physical mobile hardware before
  claiming broad mobile or assistive-technology support.

**Exit evidence:** published playtest methods and anonymized aggregate results
either support the attachment thesis or produce a smaller, explicit redesign.

## Later — earn a persistent shared world

- Explore succession and a citizen lifetime only after the first-life loop
  creates attachment; success means death creates a new decision, not a reset.
- Explore a hosted canonical region only after local playtests justify the
  operational, privacy, moderation, backup, and abuse surface.
- Explore public Chronicle pages and share replays only when factual provenance
  remains legible to a viewer with no game context.
- Explore optional provider or browser-local cognition only through bounded
  proposals; success requires better blinded player stories while the complete
  game remains compelling without a model.
- Explore additional settlements, institutions, and private world forks only
  when newcomers remain relevant in mature worlds.

Hosted services, accounts, payments, required inference, model training,
proprietary datasets, and public multiplayer are not commitments on this
roadmap.
