# Community code of conduct

EONFOLK welcomes thoughtful disagreement about games, simulation, AI, and
research. Participation must remain safe and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Be specific, patient, and honest about evidence and uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, identity, and accessibility needs.
- Give credit for others' work and disclose relevant conflicts.
- Stop an interaction when another participant asks you to disengage.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing,
impersonation, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private
information is not accepted. Neither is using project spaces to pressure people
for credentials, money, access, or off-platform contact.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Decisions consider severity, pattern,
context, and risk to affected people.

Use GitHub's built-in block and abuse-reporting tools for harassment or urgent
platform abuse. Do not put sensitive conduct details in a public issue. The
project does not yet publish a separate confidential conduct mailbox; this
limitation will be revisited before opening a larger community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project elsewhere.
